Help test Jandal before it’s on Google Play.

You’ll be installing a pre-release build. Things may break, and features may change. Your feedback is what makes the next build better.

Start with step 1

What testing means

An early build, with rough edges.

  • It’s a pre-release app. Crashes, odd answers and missing polish are expected.
  • Please stay opted in for 14 days. Google needs 12 testers opted in at the same time for a closed test to count, so leaving early can hold it up.
  • Your chats and memories stay on your phone unless you choose to share them. See the privacy notes below.

Before you start

What your phone needs.

  • Android 15 or newer.
  • 8 GB+ RAM recommended.
  • Use Wi-Fi for the first launch. About 2.7 GB to download on first launch (3.8 GB on 12 GB+ phones, which get the bigger model).

Before you join

What joining the group shares.

  • Who sees your email. Joining the Google Group shares your Google account email with the developer, who manages the group. The member list is visible to managers only, so other testers don’t see it. Google Play’s tester list shows the developer the same email.
  • What it’s used for. Only to give you access to the test and to contact you about it. It isn’t used for marketing or shared with anyone else.
  • How long it’s kept. The tester group is deleted when the beta ends. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.
  • Leaving. Opt out on the Play testing page, leave the tester group, and uninstall the app if you like.
  • The app itself. Joining doesn’t change what the app does with your data. The Privacy Policy covers that, and the Terms of Use also apply.

The app doesn’t show up in Play?

This usually means one of two things:

  • The phone’s Play Store is signed in to a different Google account from the one you used to join the group.
  • You opened the opt-in link before joining the group. Join the group first, then open the link again.

Optional

Pick an area to focus on.

Everyone uses the same sign-up links. These are just ideas for what to try, and what feedback is useful.

Voice and Hey Jandal

Hey Jandal needs to be set as your default assistant to respond to the wake word, then turn on Listen for “Hey Jandal” in Settings › Voice.

Things to try

  • Say “Hey Jandal, set a 10 minute timer”.
  • Start a voice conversation and ask a follow-up question.
  • Try it somewhere noisy, or from across the room.

Useful feedback: did it wake when you didn’t say it, did it miss you, and roughly how long did it take to answer?

Chat

The main conversation screen, with on-device answers.

Things to try

  • Ask a general question, then a follow-up that refers back to the answer.
  • Have a longer conversation, 20 messages or so, and see whether it stays on track.
  • Ask for a short piece of writing, like a message to a friend.

Useful feedback: wrong facts, answers that take a long time, and anything that freezes or stops responding.

Lists and timers

Shopping lists, named lists and timers.

Things to try

  • “Create a list called Picnic”, then “add water to my Picnic list”.
  • “Show my shopping list”, then cross an item off.
  • “Set a 10 minute timer”, “what timers do I have?”, then “cancel my timer”.

Useful feedback: items going on the wrong list, and timers that don’t go off when they should.

Feedback and privacy

Tell us what you find.

  • In the app: Settings › About › Email support.
  • By email: jandal.ai.support@gmail.com
  • Logs: nothing leaves your phone unless you choose Export logs or Email support.
  • Network use: the model download, weather, optional Nextcloud sync and Wikipedia lookups use the internet. Online speech recognition is optional and off by default; when on, your voice audio goes to your phone’s speech service. The Privacy Policy covers what’s sent and when.
