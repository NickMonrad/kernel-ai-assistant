# Join the Jandal AI beta

Jandal AI is a Kiwi AI assistant for Android. Its AI runs on your phone: once the AI model has downloaded, chat, memory, lists, notes, timers and alarms keep working in airplane mode. Some features go online only when you ask them to, such as weather, Wikipedia and currency rates, and optional Nextcloud sync for lists.

Before Jandal can go on Google Play for everyone, it needs testers to stay in a closed test for at least 14 days in a row. If you'd like to help, here's how.

## What you need

- An Android phone running **Android 15 or newer**.
- About **3 GB of free storage** and **Wi-Fi for the first run**: the app downloads its on-device AI model then (Gemma 4 E2B, about 2.4 GB), plus room for the app. Flagship phones can choose the larger E4B model (about 3.4 GB).
- A Google account, which you use to join the tester group and install from Google Play.

Older or low-memory phones may run slowly or not run the AI model at all. That's useful to hear about too.

## How to join

1. **Join the tester group:** [Jandal AI testers](TESTER_GROUP_LINK). Joining adds your Google account email to the tester list.
2. **Opt in on Google Play:** open [the Jandal AI testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kernel.ai) while signed in with the same Google account, then tap **Become a tester**.
3. **Install Jandal AI** from the Google Play link on that page. Updates arrive through Play like any other app.
4. **Stay opted in for at least 14 days in a row** and use Jandal the way you normally would. Google Play counts days you're opted in, so opting out before then (even briefly) restarts your count.

It can take a few minutes after joining the group before the Play testing page lets you opt in.

## Sending feedback

- Email [jandal.ai.support@gmail.com](mailto:jandal.ai.support@gmail.com). The developer sees your email address and anything you include.
- Or leave private feedback on the app's Google Play page while you're a tester. It goes to the developer with your Google account details.
- If you have a GitHub account, you can also [open an issue](https://github.com/NickMonrad/kernel-ai-assistant/issues). Issues are public, so don't include personal information.

## Your details

- **Who sees your email.** Joining the tester group shares your Google account email with the developer (the group's manager). The group's member list is visible to managers only, so other testers don't see it. Google Play's tester list shows the developer the same email.
- **What it's used for.** Only to give you access to the test and to contact you about it. It isn't used for marketing or shared with anyone else.
- **How long it's kept.** The tester group is deleted when the beta ends, or you can leave at any time. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.
- **The app itself.** Joining the beta doesn't change what the app does with your data; the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) covers that. The [Terms of Use](TERMS.md) also apply. The Privacy Policy doesn't cover tester sign-up; this section does.
- **Leaving.** Opt out on the Play testing page, leave the tester group, and uninstall the app if you like.

Jandal AI is open source: [github.com/NickMonrad/kernel-ai-assistant](https://github.com/NickMonrad/kernel-ai-assistant).
