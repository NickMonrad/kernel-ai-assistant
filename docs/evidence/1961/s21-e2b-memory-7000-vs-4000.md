# S21 E-2B memory: context window 7000 vs 4000 (#1961)

Device: Samsung SM-G991B (S21), Exynos 2100, Android 15, serial redacted.
Build: debug, `ai.jandal.assistant.debug`, head `84e834f343`.
Backend: E-2B on GPU. Both captures are after one chat turn.

## RAM and kill state

- MemTotal: 7,373,756 kB (about 7.03 GiB). The app reports `ram=7 GB`.
- MemAvailable during runs: about 1.33 to 1.44 GB.
- No `ai.jandal` process was killed by lmkd during either run.
- Low-memory events seen: system lmkd `Reclaim` lines for other processes near 00:56 (none were `ai.jandal`). Only kill line in that window: `com.google.android.webview`.
- No app-specific `onTrimMemory` or low-memory warning was found by grep of the app log.

## Caveats

- Swap state differs between captures. The 7000 idle capture showed about 998 MB of native heap swapped out while Dozing, so PSS and RSS are not directly comparable. Use Graphics and HeapAlloc as the primary, swap-independent fields.
- The two runs are different processes. The 4000 process was started by "Save & Restart App" (pid 2981). The 7000 process ran since engine init at 00:22 (pid 15965).
- Measured overhead of the larger window: about +170 MB Graphics and about +120 MB native HeapAlloc. Total PSS plus swap PSS differs by about 397 MB (4,071,622 vs 3,675,058 kB), which the swap noise above does not fully explain.

## Engine and budget evidence

- 7000 run: `resolved_token_cap=7000 app_context_window=7000 max_tool_result_tokens=3044`, `Generation complete`.
- 4000 run: `resolved_token_cap=4000 app_context_window=4000 max_tool_result_tokens=291`, LiteRT `prefill=2665 tokens`, `decode=49`, `Generation complete total=10032ms`.
- Restored state (verified after this run): engine init at 01:11:38 logged `maxTokens: 7000 (requested: 7000)`, `Engine ready` at 01:11:46.

## Side effects during the run (not part of the measurement)

- A mis-tap opened the Lists screen with a "New list" dialog. Cancelled. No list was created.
- A mis-tap enabled Loop (back-and-forth voice) mode for about one minute around 01:07 to 01:08. Turned off with its X control. Not measured.
- LeakCanary (debug build) reported one leak on the next launch: `VoiceInteractionService$1.this$0`, last leaked 8 hours ago. This predates these runs and is not caused by the window change. Out of scope.
