## Key finding: the Lessac parent checkpoint

Eight of the eleven voices state "Finetuned from U.S. English lessac voice (medium quality)" in their pinned card, and Southern English Female is fine-tuned from Ryan (low). The parent datasets are:

- **Lessac (Blizzard 2013)**. The [licence](https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html) is a *Research Licence Agreement* from Voice Factory International and Lessac Technologies. Clause 3.2 grants use "exclusively for Research Purposes only". "Research Purposes" expressly "excludes ... using the Materials for any commercial purpose, including the development, marketing, commercialisation, sale or licencing of voice synthesis ... products or services". The licence is non-transferable and gives no right to sub-license (cl. 3.2, 7, 8).
- **RyanSpeech**. The pinned Piper card records `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`.

The licence terms do not say whether weights fine-tuned from a model trained on this data are "based upon the Materials" for licence purposes. The definition of Research Purposes does cover use "using, incorporating or based upon the Materials". That leaves a real legal question. It falls short of the evidence bar used for Cori, which is trained from scratch on public-domain LibriVox. On the repo's conservative standard ("do not infer clearance from generic project licences"), every Lessac- or Ryan-derived voice is **HOLD** unless the owner or counsel accepts the fine-tune lineage risk. Under "conditional verdict" below, the table shows what each voice would be if that lineage were cleared.

## Sherpa-ONNX archive vs upstream (flag only; #1474)

- The Sherpa `vits-piper-en_US-joe-medium.tar.bz2` archive lists `en_US-joe-medium.onnx`, `en_US-joe-medium.onnx.json`, `MODEL_CARD`, `tokens.txt` and an `espeak-ng-data/` tree. The upstream Piper voice has only `.onnx`, `.onnx.json` and `MODEL_CARD`. So the Sherpa pack is a repackaging: it adds a converted `tokens.txt` and a bundled `espeak-ng-data` (GPL-3.0 eSpeak NG lineage, already tracked under #1474) and may carry ONNX metadata edits. The Sherpa release has no pinned upstream revision, so whether its `.onnx` matches revision `5041a7a…` is unverified. The other archives weren't listed; the same structure is assumed but not confirmed.
- Each archive carries its own `MODEL_CARD` copy. Whether it is byte-identical to the pinned upstream card was not checked.
